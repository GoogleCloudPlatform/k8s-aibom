## Steady state, v1.6.0-rc.1

Controller with default values plus `metrics.enabled=true`, three
supervised third-party kinds present (KServe, LeaderWorkerSet,
DisaggregatedSet), two tracked workloads with documents, four rolled-up
workloads. Attributed client-side over 940 s:

| Counter | Delta | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| `rest_client_requests_total` GET 200 | 51 | **3.26 requests/minute** |
| `rest_client_requests_total` PUT 200 | 1 | one status refresh |
| `controller_runtime_reconcile_total` | 2 (Deployment controller) | a pod-status event on the Pending fixture |
| `process_cpu_seconds_total` | 2.55 cpu-s | **2.7 mCPU average** |
| memory | — | 24 Mi working set (`kubectl top`) / 70 Mi RSS |

Against v1.5.1's 0.96 requests/minute with apps/v1 kinds only: the
difference is the Design 004 supervisor's liveness probe, one `Limit: 1`
list per present third-party kind every two minutes (three kinds here:
about 1.5 requests/minute), plus the watch re-establishments of the
additional informers (per-kind caches for the three kinds, CronJob,
Grove-style intermediates). This matches the CHANGELOG's stated cost
("about one request per minute on top" for AICR's two kinds) and is
independent of workload count. No writes at steady state beyond the
single status refresh.
